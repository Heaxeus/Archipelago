# Click Mage

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every quest is now a check. Depending on YAML settings, mining a resource, building a structure, or crafting an item can also be a check.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Depending on your YAML settings, potential items could be:
- Resource nodes
- Upgrades
- Blueprints

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

You will need to use the AP Text Client to see items sent out and received.
